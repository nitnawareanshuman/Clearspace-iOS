Privacy policy

Contact support

Email anshumannitnaware7@gmail.com for help with PipSweep, bug reports, or feedback.

When reporting a problem, include your iOS version, PipSweep version, and the steps that led to the problem. Avoid sharing private photos, contact details or calendar contents.

Review your library

After scanning, open a category to preview and select media. Swipe mode offers Keep, Remove and Undo. Nothing is deleted until you approve the final review and system confirmation.

Recover photos or videos

Open Photos and find Recently Deleted under Utilities. Unlock the album if asked, select your items, and choose Recover. PipSweep cannot recover permanently deleted media or undo contact/calendar changes.

Apple's deletion and recovery guide

Storage estimates

Estimated media removed is different from immediately recovered device capacity. Recently Deleted, iCloud settings and unavailable sizes can affect the result. Your app's Settings contains more guidance.

Permissions and missing content

Limited access shows only shared photos or contacts. Cloud-only media is not downloaded for scanning. You can change permissions in iPhone Settings, return to PipSweep, and scan again.
